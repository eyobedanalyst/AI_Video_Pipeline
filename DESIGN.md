# DESIGN.md

## Audience and intent
- **Audience:** my family and friends, watching on a phone (WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok), so the main version is vertical 9:16.
- **Intent:** after the film they feel respect and gratitude for the unseen daily work of mothers.
- **Story:** one AI-generated mother's day (prayer, family, church, home, dinner, dishes, sleep). Based on my own mother's daily life. No real face or voice is used.
- **Why first person:** a son or daughter telling the story feels warmer and more personal than a neutral narrator.
- **Motif:** her hands (prayer, lunch, holding a hand, dishes, prayer again). Opening and closing on the same image gives the film a shape.

## User test 1: radio cut (narration over black screen)
Three people from my audience listened and answered three questions.

| Person | What it was about (their words) | Favourite line | Confusing / too long |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1: father (her husband) | A woman who gives everything to her family and to people outside the home. "I sometimes enjoy the things she does without thinking about the effort behind them." | "She wraps my father's lunch the way she does everything: carefully, with her whole heart." | The evangelist and listening part was a little long; shorten it so viewers focus on her daily sacrifices. |
| P2: sister (daughter) | Mothers work from early morning to late at night without time for themselves; we enjoy life while she is still working. | "We eat and laugh, and never ask how tired she is." | The beginning was slow; the first prayer scene could be shorter. Wanted to reach the family scenes sooner. |
| P3: family friend | A tribute to all mothers, not just one woman; faith, family and community. | "My mother is one woman. But she is every mother in this country." | The change from church to coming home was unclear. The ending should have more time to be emotional. |

### What worked (kept unchanged)
All three understood the intended feeling, and each picked a different favourite line from the family and "every mother" parts. Shots 3, 8 and 10 narration kept as they were.

### What I changed because of it
| Feedback | Change in storyboard.json |
|---|---|
| Beginning too slow (P2) | s01 narration cut from 22 to 14 words; s01 duration 9s to 7s |
| Evangelist part too long (P1) | s05 narration shortened (removed "the tired, the grieving"); duration 10s to 7s |
| Church-to-home transition unclear (P3) | s06 narration now says "as evening falls, she carries them home"; duration 7s to 8s |
| Ending needs more time (P3) | s10 duration 11s to 14s so the last line can breathe; end card 3s to 4s |

### Limits of this test
All three listeners are close to the story (husband, daughter, friend). Their reaction may be kinder than a stranger's. For test 2 I will include at least one person outside the family.

Test 2 repeated test 1's three complaints (slow opening, long church part, unclear transition). I made real changes: s01 cut from 14 to 10 words and 7s to 5s, s05 reduced to one short sentence, and s06 now states "when the last person has gone, she walks home". I kept the family dinner and the ending, because all three viewers named them as favourites.
